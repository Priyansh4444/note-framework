Original generative ink · no two marks alike

About this study

Three landscapes, drawn from the same 2,400 brush marks. Rain becomes branches; branches find their way into a circle. Every mark is generated locally from a fixed seed. This is an original Canvas study inspired by Bee’s ink-wash experiment, with no video or external artwork.

Play moves through the scenes once. Choose any scene, or use Previous and Next, at any time. Reduced motion presents a still for each scene. Motion sleeps when this landscape leaves the screen.
