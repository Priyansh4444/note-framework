Inside the linkage

Inverse kinematics means finding joint angles from a desired tool position. This drawing solves a two-link triangle with the cosine rule. The same numerical model positions every joint and supplies the measured reach. Link lengths stay at 220 and 175 mm; the tool is limited to a radius of 115–380 mm and an angle of 65–150° around the shoulder. That working sector keeps every pose in view.

Named targets use a critically damped response: each new command continues from the displayed position and velocity. Dragging and keyboard moves respond immediately. Reduced motion settles targets immediately; leaving the page pauses the clock.

An original SVG study inspired by the tangible articulation in Fuma Nama’s mechanical-arm clip. No reference artwork or character is included. This is a geometric interaction, not a mechanical safety simulation.
